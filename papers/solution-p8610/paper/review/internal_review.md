# Internal manuscript review

Review date: 2026-08-28.

## Verdict

The manuscript is a coherent, self-contained substantial-partial-result
paper. It disproves universal surjectivity of the unrestricted fixed-parameter
Sinh--Gordon K-transform by an all-level pure-$A_4$ target at $b=1/4$, and it
classifies every nonempty integer-spin fiber as an affine translate of an
explicit parity-separated inverse-limit kernel. It does not claim
nonsurjectivity at generic coupling, classification under a growth bound, or
compatibility of normal jets. Those boundaries agree in the title, abstract,
theorem statements, proofs, scope section, and conclusion.

## Proof audit

- Separate $2\pi i$-periodicity and common-boost covariance force every
  noninteger-spin source and target family to vanish. The fiber theorem is
  therefore stated only for integer spin, while the noninteger branch is
  treated separately and exactly.
- After $z_j=e^{\beta_j}$, the rapidity torus modulo common scaling and the
  finite permutation group is Stein. Equal-rapidity poles cancel pairwise in
  the transform row. Clearing the annihilation divisor gives the finite
  coherent row $A_x$, whose image and syzygy sheaves are respectively the
  fixed-particle target ideal and exact kernel.
- On each reduced annihilation component, quotienting labelled boundary
  values by the four-dimensional tail-independent space $W_e$ expresses the
  recurrence as a quotient equality. It retains shared, disjoint, and higher
  intersections and imposes values rather than normal jets.
- Restricting the quotient map to $\ker A_x$ and choosing a finite syzygy
  presentation gives $C_x=B_xK_x$. Thus a prescribed lower kernel section
  lifts exactly when its boundary datum lies in $\operatorname{im}C_x$, and
  all local choices differ by $K_x(\ker C_x)$.
- Cartan theorem B globalizes every locally admitted one-step lift. Taking
  retained paths separately through the even and odd particle numbers gives
  the complete zero-transform family space. Subtracting one lift proves that
  every nonempty fiber is its affine translate; this statement neither
  assumes nor asserts that every target fiber is nonempty.
- Pillin's convention is matched explicitly by $B=4b$ and
  $F_{\mathrm P}^{\min}=F/F(i\pi)$. Removing the minimal factors from the
  polynomial ansatz gives the manuscript's scalar $\mathcal K_n$, including
  the required power of $F(i\pi)$. Pillin's all-level recursion, rather than
  a finite-prefix extrapolation, supplies the pure-$A_4$ family with
  $\mathcal K_0=\mathcal K_2=0$ and $\mathcal K_4=1$.
- At $z^*=(-1,1,-i,i)$ the homogeneous recurrent four-particle module is
  generated by $1$, four $d_{12}e_C$, four $d_{34}e_A$, and sixteen
  $d_{12}d_{34}e_Ae_C$ terms. The forward zero-lower residue identity makes
  their transforms holomorphic on both active divisors, so evaluation of
  these 25 generators controls the entire local module. Every regular value
  is zero.
- The symmetry-closed lower two-particle block has kernel equal to the
  label-constant line unless $g_{00}=g_{11}=\kappa$. The square minors
  $(g_{00}-\kappa)^2$ and $(g_{11}-\kappa)^2$ cover the determinant-zero
  nonconstant locus. At the constant pair, the two displayed kernel
  directions also induce zero four-particle value; the tempting sinh
  direction gives $-2i$ but violates the shared-pair recurrence by $-i$.
  Hence no fixed diagonal pair lifts the pure-$A_4$ target.
- The generic-coupling control evaluates an explicit global homogeneous
  recurrence-zero correction as $4096ia^3c^4$. Its nonzero value for
  $c\ne0$ shows why the $b=1/4$ point-value obstruction cannot be promoted to
  a generic-$b$ theorem by the same argument.

## Literature and source audit

- Fresh primary-source searches covered the Sinh--Gordon bootstrap converse,
  polynomial form-factor families, descendant constructions, multipoint
  correlation functions, and Wightman reconstruction. No current primary
  source located in the audit resolves the unrestricted surjectivity or fiber
  question answered here.
- Kozlowski's 2021/2023 source proves the forward binary-labelled transform
  and leaves the all-solutions correspondence open. The 2025
  Kozlowski--Simon multipoint and Wightman works continue to use forward
  constructions and add analytic or growth requirements; they do not supply
  this no-growth converse.
- Pillin's 1998 source was checked at the equation level for the all-level
  polynomial recursion and its independent kernel components. The sources of
  Fring--Mussardo--Simonetti, Koubek--Mussardo, and Feigin--Lashkevich were
  checked for neighboring solution classes and normalization context.
- `cocite.py --new` and `cocite.py --intro` identified Smirnov and
  Karowski--Weisz as foundational omissions; both were added. Cartan's
  quotient and theorem-B sources were checked against the exact analytic
  globalization used in the proof.
- All cited bibliography keys agree between the canonical MatrixLab
  bibliography and the paper-local bibliography.
